Scalars and vectors are both examples of quantities. Both have magnitudes, but vectors have direction.

Since vectors have magnitude and direction, we can draw them as arrows.
```desmos-graph
grid=false;
hideAxisNumbers=false;
left=0.1;right=4;
bottom=0.1;top=4;

---
y = \{ 0<x < 3 : x\} | #fd96a9
y = \{ 2<x < 3 : 3\} | #fd96a9
x = \{ 2<y < 3 : 3\} | #fd96a9
```

Two vectors that exist in the same 2D space are described as _coplanar_.

Resistance of a conductor is effected by many factors:
$$
R=\frac{\rho L}{A}
$$
where:
- $\rho$ is specific resistivity (a material constant), typically measured in $\Omega m$ $[\mathsf{M}\mathsf{L}^3\mathsf{T}^{-3}\mathsf{I}^{-2}]$
- $L$ is length of conductor $[\mathsf{L}]$
- $A$ is cross sectional area normal to the flow of charge $[\mathsf{L}^2]$

### Conductivity
Conductivity $\sigma \space[\mathsf{M}^{-1}\mathsf{L}^{-3}\mathsf{T}^3\mathsf{I}^2]$, is defined by the reciprocal of resistance:
$$\sigma=\frac{1}{R}$$

To have a potential difference across a circuit, a source must apply an *electromotive force* to induce a [[260914 Electric potential|potential difference]].

### Difference between potential difference and electromotive force
EMF is the "cause" for potential difference; a source has constant EMF, whilst potential difference changes throughout a circuit. There can be EMF and no current, but there cannot be voltage and no current.

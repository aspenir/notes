Screw gauge micrometers are similar to vernier calipers, except they:
- Have a ratcheting mechanism that prevents objects from being squashed by measurement
- Are free from Abbé error (or sine error) due to flexing in the sliding jaw not being magnified by length
- Use a screw thread to move the measuring tip, making it easier to finely adjust (compared to a caliper, which can slip)

Micrometers can have a zero error due to wear, meaning an actual value of 0 results in a negative measurement.

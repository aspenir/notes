Motion is the movement of objects. It is defined mathematically by the derivatives of position with respect to time, with the most common three variables being:

- Displacement - $s\ \ \ \ [\mathsf{L}]$
- Velocity - $v=\frac{ds}{dt} \ \ \ \ [\mathsf{L}\mathsf{T}^{-1}]$
- Acceleration - $a=\frac{dv}{dt}=\frac{d^2s}{dt^2} \ \ \ \ [\mathsf{L}\mathsf{T}^{-2}]$

### Relative motion
When an object moves with another of similar speed, sometimes it is more helpful to compare their velocities in a _frame of reference_ with another - given two objects $A$ and $B$, we can calculate the _relative velocity_ of $A$ from the perspective of $B$:
$$
V_{A|B}=V_{A}-V_{B}
$$
### SUVAT derivations
Given $v=u+at$ and $s=\frac{u+v}{2}t$, we can derive the rest of SUVAT equations:

$$\begin{align}
s&=\frac{u+(u+at)}{2}t \\
s&=ut+\frac{1}{2}at^2
\end{align}
$$

$$\begin{align}
v&=u+at \\
u&=v-at
\end{align}$$

$$\begin{align}
s&=\frac{(v-at)+v}{2}t \\
&=t\left(v-\frac{1}{2}at\right)
\end{align}$$

$$\begin{align}
\begin{cases}

s&=\frac{u+v}{2}t \\
t&=\frac{v-u}{a}
\end{cases}
 \\
s&=\left(\frac{u+v}{2}\right)\left(\frac{v-u}{a}\right) \\
&=\frac{(u+v)(v-u)}{2a} \\
&=\frac{v^2-u^2}{2a} \\ \\
2as&=v^2-u^2 \\
v^2=u^2+2as
\end{align}$$

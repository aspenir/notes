6. The curve of $y=2^x$ is transformed by:
- a translation in the positive $x$ direction by 4 units
- a stretch with scale factor 8 parallel to the $y$ axis
- a reflection across the $y$ axis
Find the equation of the image, giving your equation in the form of $y=2^{f(x)}$

$$\begin{align}
y&=8(2^{-(x-4)})
=2^3\times 2^{-x+4} \\
&=2^{-x+7}
\end{align}$$

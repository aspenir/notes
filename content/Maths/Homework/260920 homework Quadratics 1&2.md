4. Find $a$ and $b$ such that $x^{2}+6x+20\equiv(x+a)^{2}+b$, and hence find the vertex of $x^{2}+6x+20$ and greatest value of $\frac{1}{x^{2}+6x+20}$:











$$
\begin{aligned}
x^{2}+6x+20&=(x+3)^{2}-9+20 \\
&=(x+3)^{2}-11 \\
a&=3 \\
b&=-11 \\
 \\
\therefore \text{Vertex is at}\space &(-3,-11) \\

&=\frac{1}{11}
\end{aligned}
$$
